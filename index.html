AbilityOne, and how Envision is built around it.

Federal Procurement Vehicle

The federal channel that brings qualified contracts to Envision.

AbilityOne is a federal program that establishes procurement priority for contracts performed by qualified nonprofit agencies employing people who are blind or have significant disabilities. It is a recognized contract vehicle, structured by law, governed by federal oversight.

For Envision, AbilityOne is not a credential. It is the operating environment.

01 What it does

Creates a federal procurement pathway

Federal buyers source goods and services from AbilityOne agencies under a recognized contracting framework, governed by the U.S. AbilityOne Commission and supported by NIB.

02 What it requires

Qualified employment standards

AbilityOne work must be performed by a workforce that is at least 75 percent employees who are blind or have significant disabilities. Envision exceeds that threshold.

03 How Envision fits

One of the largest qualified agencies

One of the largest and most capable AbilityOne agencies in the country, with active federal contracts, NIB-aligned compliance, and DOD program experience.

04 What it means here

A real channel into federal medical supply

For Steris, partnering with Envision creates direct exposure to a procurement pathway that is structurally important for federal healthcare contracts.

Pathway three, future state

ORLocate, and the domestic supply opportunity.

ORLocate System Future state

Steris-only RFID surgical counting

ORLocate is a Steris-only system that uses RFID-tagged surgical gauze and sponges to enhance counting accuracy and reduce the risk of retained surgical items. The supporting consumables are a Steris-branded, Steris-tagged product line.

  • Designed to align with AORN counting guidelines
  • HF RFID handheld and locator hardware
  • Over 77 SKUs of tagged sponges, gauze, and towels
